- Tester findings reports may carry `deferred` (at most 12): `{ id, class,
  location, note }`, with `class` one of `pre-existing`, `polish`,
  `unclear-requirement` or `duplicate` (a `duplicate` names the id it
  duplicates in its note). `findings` holds blocking defects only, a `PASS`
  may carry deferred items, and they are kept in the checkpoint and listed in
  the review, never dropped or repaired. An `unclear-requirement` at the
  current head makes `orchestrate stage --advance` checkpoint `State:
  human-gate`, `Gate stage: review` and `Gate: unclear requirement: <ids>`
  before any repair or done, recording the ids under `Gated requirements`;
  once that gate is resolved, the next selection is a fresh Tester review told
  the decision, and listing a gated id as unclear again is refused.
- Worker protocols converge. Implementation and resume briefs require a
  Developer self-check of each acceptance criterion against the diff before
  `DONE`, recorded as `Self-check: <command> | head <sha> | exit 0 | criteria
  <n>/<n>`. A repair fixes every blocking finding of the report in one
  session, adds or names a regression check per finding id, and records
  `Self-check: ... | addressed <ids>`. After a completed repair the Adversary
  brief names the repaired ids and the range `<reviewed sha>..<head>`, and
  requires a fresh regression experiment per repaired id and at least one on
  the repair diff, never citing the previous round's experiments. The review
  brief names the resolved finding ids. The Tester's full current-head review
  is unchanged.
