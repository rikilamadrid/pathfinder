- The orchestration engine holds the repair budget. `orchestrate stage
  --advance` writes `Repair rounds: <n>` when it selects a repair of a new
  reviewed head (a pending or restarted repair of the same head is not counted
  again). In place of a third round, or of any round when a claim has a
  `Repair:` line but no count, it checkpoints `State: human-gate`,
  `Gate stage: repair` and `Gate: repair budget exhausted after <n> rounds:
  <finding ids>` (or `repair round count unknown: <finding ids>`) and returns
  no session; resolving that gate resumes only the one counted repair.
  `orchestrate start` no longer asks the human for a repair count.
- Tester findings carry a stable `id` (`^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]{0,47}$`), required
  when writing through `findings.mjs --checkpoint` and optional when reading a
  legacy report. A completed repair's ids join `Resolved findings`, and a
  checkpoint write that names a resolved id is refused unless that finding
  carries `reopened: { because, evidence }`.
